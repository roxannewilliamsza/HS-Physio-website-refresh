Body & mind

Stress lives in the body, too.

Many people come in with sore, tight muscles and no obvious injury. Often the root cause is stress, anxiety or burnout — the body holds on to what the mind is carrying: a stiff neck and shoulders, a clenched jaw, headaches, restless sleep.

Physio isn’t always the first thing people think of for mental wellbeing, but hands-on treatment, breathwork and gentle movement can help settle your nervous system and ease that physical load.

  • Soft-tissue release for tension that stress holds in the body
  • Simple breathing and relaxation techniques to use at home
  • Gentle movement and Pilates to rebuild trust in your body

This complements — never replaces — care from your psychologist, counsellor or GP.

    Book online or give me a call. Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.

About

Hi, I’m Hayley.

I’ll find what’s really causing your pain, treat it hands-on, and give you the tools to stay well — so you can get back to the things you love.

  • Physiotherapy degree, University of the Western Cape, 2008
  • Community service at Alexandra Hospital, working with physical and cognitive disabilities
  • Four years in the Hand & Upper Limb Orthopaedic Unit at Constantiaberg Mediclinic and Vincent Pallotti
